A Warehousing Partner That Isn't a Faceless Operator

Grandmoor is a Mississauga-based 3PL built around direct, hands-on involvement — from the people running the business to the team handling your inventory.

Grandmoor was built on a simple observation about the 3PL industry: the bigger a warehousing operator gets, the harder it becomes to reach a real person who actually knows your account. Established Mississauga-area operators compete on decades of history, in-house fleets, and multi-city coverage. Grandmoor competes differently — on responsiveness, direct involvement from the people who run the business, and a level of attention that's hard to maintain once an operator scales past a certain size.

Hands-On Partnership

Grandmoor isn't a faceless logistics operator. Our founding team is directly involved in day-to-day operations, sales, and account management, so you always have a real point of contact who understands your business — not a ticket number in a support queue.

Operational Transparency

Clear reporting, defined SLAs, and a team that treats your inventory like it's our own. No black-box logistics — you know what's happening with your goods at every stage.

Built to Scale With You

From single-pallet storage to full-facility dedicated space, our operation is structured to grow alongside your volume — without forcing you into rigid, one-size-fits-all contracts.

Strategically Located

Our Mississauga facility offers direct highway access to the GTA, Southwestern Ontario, and cross-border routes, cutting transit time to your customers and suppliers.

Why We're Based in Mississauga

Mississauga sits at the centre of Ontario's logistics network — home to Toronto Pearson International Airport and within easy reach of Highways 401, 403, 407, and the QEW. That's not incidental to how Grandmoor operates; it's the reason the facility is where it is, giving clients across the GTA fast access in and out regardless of what they're storing or shipping.

A newer, single-facility operator has to earn trust differently than an established competitor with decades of history. Our approach is to be direct about what we do well, realistic about what we're still building toward, and available when clients need to reach someone who actually knows their account.
