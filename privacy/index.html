Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: August 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Grandmoor Inc. ("Grandmoor," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website at grandmoor.ca (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through the Site, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you. We handle personal information in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy laws.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two main ways:

Information you provide directly

When you submit the Contact form, the Qualify questionnaire, or otherwise reach out to us, we may collect your name, company name, job title, email address, phone number, company location, and details about your warehousing, fulfillment, or logistics needs that you choose to share with us.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the Site automatically collects certain technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and referring URLs. We use Google Tag Manager to manage tags on the Site, which may in turn load analytics or marketing tools that use cookies or similar technologies to collect this information. The Contact page also embeds a Google Maps view, which may set its own cookies when loaded.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to quote requests and inquiries submitted through the Site;
  • Understand a prospective customer's storage, fulfillment, and logistics needs so we can recommend an appropriate service;
  • Communicate with you about your inquiry, including by phone or email;
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the Site; and
  • Understand aggregate trends in how the Site is used.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and Analytics

We use Google Tag Manager, which may enable cookies or similar tracking technologies to help us understand how visitors use the Site. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings; doing so may affect how parts of the Site function. Third-party services we embed or load, such as Google Maps and any tools deployed through Google Tag Manager, are subject to their own privacy policies and cookie practices, which we do not control.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Site and process form submissions, such as our website hosting and form-processing provider (Netlify) and Google (for embedded maps and tag management);
  • Professional advisors, where necessary for legal, accounting, or similar purposes; and
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

If you go on to become a customer, information you provide may also be used in connection with your service agreement, as described in that agreement.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information collected through the Site for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, maintain a record of our communications, and comply with our legal and accounting obligations, after which it is deleted or de-identified.

7. Your Rights

Under PIPEDA, you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request that we correct inaccurate information, and withdraw your consent to our collection, use, or disclosure of your information (subject to legal or contractual restrictions). To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10 below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the personal information we hold from loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
